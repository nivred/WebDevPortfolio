Bowling - $25 per person 6PM

Two lanes, 6 people per lane, and one full hour of bowling.

Bring your A-game, your lucky socks, or whatever ritual you use to convince yourself that gutter balls are intentional.

WhirlyBall - $25 per person 8PM

WhirlyBall is $25 for one hour, played in 10-minute rounds.

Only 10 people can play at a time, so please be prepared to rotate in and, most importantly, be a good human and give someone else a turn when needed.
